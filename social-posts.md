# Facebook posts — ready to use

15 posts written from the photos and videos already in this repo. Two a week
covers about two months. Nothing here needs to be shot or bought.

Facts used in these: 47 years (Skaggs 1979, SoCal Bobcat 2002), CA license
#834117, 714-787-9970, crews 24/7, office Mon–Fri 7–5, Anaheim based, all of
Southern California, ISNetworld member.

**Voice rule:** these are written contractor-to-contractor. Short sentences,
no hype, no exclamation points, no stock-photo energy. A superintendent
scrolling past should think "those guys know what they're doing," not
"that's an ad." Change any wording that doesn't sound like you — it's better
in your words than in mine.

**Anything in [brackets] is a blank to fill.** City names and project types
are the single biggest credibility upgrade. "Wall sawing a dock pit" is fine.
"Wall sawing a dock pit in Ontario" is better. Only name a GC in writing if
you have their OK.

## Notes

**Hashtags.** They do close to nothing on Facebook. If you want them, three
is plenty and put them on the last line: `#concretecutting #coredrilling
#socal`. Instagram is the opposite — there they actually help, so use ten to
fifteen there.

**Best times to post.** Weekday mornings, 6–8 AM. Your audience is checking
their phone before the gate opens, not at lunch.

**The one thing that would double the value of all of this:** put the city
and the job type on every post. Fifteen posts that say "wall sawing" get
scrolled past. Fifteen that say "wall sawing a dock pit in Ontario" tell a
super in Ontario that you work near him. That's the whole game.

**Reposting.** Everything here works on Instagram and TikTok too — you have
accounts on both (@socalbobcatandskaggs, @socalbobcat). Same photo, same
caption, more hashtags on Instagram.
